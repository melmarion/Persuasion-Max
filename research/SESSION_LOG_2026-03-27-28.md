# Session Log — March 27-28, 2026

## What Was Built (in order)

### Persuasion-Max Core Engine (from scratch)
1. **AppraisalExtractor** — 3 modes (heuristic/ollama/claude), 7 cognitive appraisal dimensions from Smith & Ellsworth 1985. Context-aware (11 UX contexts). 3,608-char system prompt with operational definitions at 5 calibration points per dimension. Negation-aware heuristic with `\b` word boundaries.
2. **CircuitPredictor** — 3 competing circuits (approach/avoidance/deliberation) with 32 documented weights. 3 interaction terms FITTED from real data (valence × goal_relevance, valence × agency, novelty × coping). 3 time horizons: immediate_compliance, repeat_compliance, retaliation_probability. All weights labeled CALIBRATED/CONSTRAINED/UNCALIBRATED with citations.
3. **SomaticMarkerStore** — Damasio's hypothesis as persistent key-value store. Temporal decay (30-day half-life). Reinforcement on reactivation. Reconsolidation window computation.
4. **LimbicCascade** — 6-stage pipeline orchestrator. Single `analyze()` call chains everything.
5. **ReframingEngine** — Rewritten from editorial prescriptions to mechanical tradeoff surface. Reports consequences of changing each dimension across all 3 time horizons. No "do this, don't do that."
6. **SequenceAnalyzer** — THE NOVEL CONTRIBUTION. Models multi-step flows as trajectories through 7D appraisal space. Computes momentum, conflict spikes, dopamine prediction error chains, somatic marker accumulation. PCA projection. Nobody has published this.
7. **UX Pattern Library** — 26 patterns from real products (Duolingo, Stripe, Notion, etc.) tagged by circuit and dimension.
8. **Technique-to-Appraisal Bridge** — 22 auditor techniques mapped to dimension shifts.

### Mechanical Extensions (6 new capabilities)
9. **Agency Decomposition** — Splits coercive vs normative agency reduction. Same agency=0.15: coercive → insula 0.50, retaliation 2.7x. Normative → insula 0.00, retaliation 1.0x.
10. **Threat-Action Coupling** — Avoidance → compliance when the prescribed action IS the escape behavior. Avoidance 0.70 uncoupled → 0% compliance. Coupled → 79%.
11. **Deliberation Suppression** — Computed from temporal pressure + cognitive load + emotional arousal. With default option: suppressed deliberation routes to default compliance.
12. **Somatic Marker Overwrite Protocol** — Reconsolidation mechanics (Nader et al. 2000). Computes reactivation intensity, window, minimum prediction error.
13. **Prediction Error Engineering** — Optimal expectation-reality gaps per sequence transition.
14. **Loss Frame Converter** — Gain→loss reframing with predicted compliance delta (Kahneman & Tversky ~2x).

## Empirical Findings (Real Data)

### Persuasion for Good Regression (N=1,017)

| Extraction | AUC | Best Finding |
|-----------|-----|--------------|
| Heuristic (N=1,017) | 0.577 | Valence is strongest linear predictor (0.159) |
| Ollama llama3.2 (N=200) | 0.604 | Interactions dominate linear terms |
| Ollama llama3.2 (N=50) | 0.654 | novelty × coping = 0.264 (largest signal) |
| Combined 19-feature (N=1,017) | 0.589 | Linguistic features add +0.012 marginal lift |
| Linguistic only (N=1,017) | 0.525 | Near chance — surface features don't predict in PFG |

### Weights Confirmed vs Contradicted

| Dimension | Hand-tuned | Fitted (Ollama N=50) | Status |
|-----------|-----------|---------------------|--------|
| valence | 0.300 | 0.345 | CONFIRMED — even stronger than assumed |
| agency | 0.100 | 0.142 | CONFIRMED — matters more than assumed |
| coping_potential | 0.200 | 0.131 | CONFIRMED — direction right, magnitude lower |
| novelty | -0.150 (penalty) | +0.085 | **CONTRADICTED** — novelty HELPS in charity context |
| temporal_proximity | 0.000 | -0.059 | CONFIRMED negative — urgency hurts in charity |
| goal_relevance | 0.250 | 0.054 | Weak — much smaller than assumed |
| certainty | 0.150 | 0.064 | Weak — smaller than assumed |

### Interaction Effects (THE HEADLINE FINDING)

Interaction terms are LARGER than linear terms. This confirms the multiplicative hypothesis:

| Interaction | Weight (Heuristic) | Weight (Ollama) |
|------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| valence × goal_relevance | 0.147 | 0.235 |
| valence × agency | 0.136 | 0.132 |
| novelty × coping | — | 0.264 (largest) |
| novelty × certainty | — | -0.178 (antagonistic) |

**Implication:** The current additive formulas miss the dominant signal. Interaction terms should be added to all circuit formulas. This has been partially done (3 terms in approach circuit) but the avoidance and deliberation circuits still lack interaction terms.

### Persona Differentiation (Ollama extraction required)

| Test | Result |
|------|--------|
| Impulse buyer vs skeptic on sale stimulus | 80% vs 50% = 29.7pp spread |
| Liberal vs conservative on empathy appeal | 56% vs 48% = +8pp (correct direction) |
| Liberal vs conservative on authority appeal | 45% vs 53% = -8pp (correct direction) |
| Heuristic extraction | No differentiation (techniques not detected) |

**Critical finding:** Persona differentiation ONLY works with LLM extraction. The heuristic detector doesn't find enough techniques in natural text to trigger the modulations. Setting `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` or using Ollama is required for the recipient model to produce meaningful differences.

## Honest Limitations

1. **AUC 0.577-0.654 on real data.** Better than chance (0.50) but not better than a junior marketer's intuition (~0.75). The bottleneck is extraction quality, not architecture.

2. **Zero deliberation predictions in heuristic mode.** The confusion matrix shows 0 deliberation classifications because regex produces ~0.5 for all dimensions, which never shifts dominance away from approach.

3. **Single-domain calibration.** All fitted weights come from charity persuasion (Persuasion for Good). These weights are WRONG for e-commerce, political messaging, and ad copy. Domain-specific calibration is the #1 priority.

4. **TRIBE v2 alignment (r=0.776) is internal consistency, not external validation.** The reference activation values were hand-assigned from the paper, not computed by running TRIBE v2.

5. **The CONSTRAINED label is generous.** "Bounded by published data" means "I read a paper and picked a number that doesn't contradict it." The citation creates an illusion of empirical grounding.

6. **Interaction terms were fitted on N=50-200 with Ollama.** Small samples overfit. The N=200 AUC (0.604) is more honest than N=50 (0.654).
